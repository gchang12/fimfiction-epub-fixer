A PortalSparkle AU Story

“…And then it was like, I got mad at her! Understandably so, I hope! So I reaffirmed that no, you and I are not dating. We’re just friends!”

“Wow, your roommate really sounds like a hoof-full.”

The two ponies chuckle.

Roughly a week has passed since that fateful day Portal Bump and Twilight Sparkle’s lives crossed paths. Over that time, the two have made it a habit to find time to spend with each other - a new friend means a lot to learn about them, and a lot to get used to.

“Well, Syntie is nice at the end of the day, y’know?” Portal Bump says, leaning back into a pile of pillows among the many strewn across the second floor of the Golden Oak Library. “She likes to be mean, but it’s all in good fun - she always means well, understands who I am.” The pegasus continues, hoof against her chest.

“Syntax Error sure sounds like an interesting mare, but you’re right. The fact she let you move in with her at such a tumultuous time tells me a lot about the friendship between you two.”

The unicorn levitates a scroll and quill over, jotting down some notes.

“I presume that counts towards your friendship studies for the Princess, right?” Portal asks, pointing a hoof at the unicorn. She’s met with an ‘mmhm!’ from the unicorn, who continues on: “Part of my studies involve reporting what I’ve learnt about friendship, so I figured I’d get some notes down about the one between you and your roommate.”

The pegasus chuckles. “Just wait till you find out Syntie’s my ex-girlfriend. Wonder how that knowledge affects your notes.”

Twilight’s eyes widen, this totally changes what she understands of the two’s friendship. She moves closer to the pegasus instinctively - “Really?! I-“

“It was brief! It wasn’t for very long!” The pegasus raises their hooves in a surrendering position, giggling. “It’s probably part of why she’s been teasing me about us being a… thing.”


Beep-beep-beep! Beep-beep-beep!


The sound of a stopwatch going off alerts the two ponies, who quickly glance over at the source of the beeping. Twilight uses her magic to grab hold of the stopwatch, resetting the timer and letting the countdown begin again.

“And now we swap who leads the conversation again! My turn!” Twilight gleefully exclaims, tapping her two forehooves together.

“You were right; this is a very fun solution to make sure we both get enough time to talk!” Portal responds, tapping her hooves together as well.

“I do wish we had Spike right now to handle the Stopwatch Timer for us, but considering the margin of error his claws bring about when it comes to timing things, I’m fine to take over.”

Portal glances around the library.

“Where is he, anyways?”

“Off at Canterlot on some royal business.”


The pegasus chooses to not question it - she loved hearing the stories the unicorn had to tell her about her life, her studies, and her adventures, but Portal isn’t exactly intent on finding out at this moment how a little dragon like Spike somehow has royal business to tend to - she’s got bigger priorities right now.

Maybe how that all works can be a question for another day.


“Right. Understood.”

“Spike is really the best little brother I could’ve asked for, he hasn’t got the smarts that I do-“ Portal smirks at Twilight as she mentions her intelligence, half-lidding her eyes. “-But having somepony who looks at things from a different perspective keeps me humble.”

“He is kind of adorable, too.”

Twilight seems to get embarrassed on Spike’s behalf. “Y-Yea, he is just a little dragon still.”

“I’ve been meaning to ask about that, actually. You said you hatched him as part of your entrance exam to Celestia’s School for Gifted Unicorns, right? Do you… know where his egg came from?”

The unicorn stops for a moment to ponder.

“Well, I did ask Celestia one time, but-“

The sound of distant thunder interrupts the egghead duo. They glance over at an upper window to see storm clouds having gathered, blocking out the sky.

“Oh gosh, I forgot that the Pegasi were scheduled to prepare some downfall today! I was considering asking them if I could help out…”

“It’s fine, Portal. Could always help out another time!”

Portal Bump nods, she gets up from the pillows and goes over to her bag. As her wing reaches out to grab it, Twilight asks: “Where are you going?”

The pegasus turns around. “I figured I would head back home because of the storm, you know? My bag is waterproof, but I’d rather not get caught in the rain, so I best get going now before—-”


CRASH.


The sound of even closer lightning causes Portal to jump a little, the two mares glance at the window again to find the downpour of rain has begun. The pegasus sighs.

“Hope you don’t mind if I stick around then?”

“I don’t mind! It’s still my turn as per the stopwatch too.” Twilight smiles, Portal nods and trots back over to her claimed pillows, flopping back into it as she folds her hooves across her chest.

The storm outside continues to build, the wind beginning to howl and get stronger while loose leaves are sent flying through the air.

“Heh. Nice and dry under here! Sorta.”

“Ooh! Unacceptable!”

Applejack has found herself taking shelter from the storm under a picnic table. Despite her earlier notion to Rarity that she could join her, the unicorn refuses to - all because she doesn’t want her hooves to get muddy.

“Rarity, ‘ah swear, unless you’ve got another idea, we’ll be stuck needing to camp out in this here storm!”

The unicorn scoffs.

“I simply refuse! I will not muddy my hooves! You wouldn’t know how to keep your coat clean like I do - style matters, darling.”

The earth pony smirks. “…And yet for a pony who prides ‘emselves on beauty, you sure would rather stand in the rain than come on in here under some cover. Weren’t you complainin’ earlier all about how the downpour would ruin your…” Applejack raises two hooves to make air quotes, “Beautifully styled mane?”

“Sweetie, a ruined mane is one thing, my coat is another matter entirely!” Rarity continues to clarify her point, all the while the downpour and wind continue to get worse around the two.

“We wouldn’t even be in this here predicament had you not been so focused on making all the pulled branches look pretty! Priorities, Rares! Learn ‘em!” Applejack shouts at the unicorn. “Well maybe you, dear, need to learn some priorities yourself, hm!” Rarity replies, the two glaring at each other.

Applejack looks around, attempting to break the conversation lest it turns into another standoff where the two try to continually one-up each other. The earth pony catches sight of the Golden Oak Library in the distance - Twilight is very likely home at this moment, Applejack realises, and she’s certain the unicorn would welcome her and Rarity in to ride out the downpour.

“Rarity, we can’t be sittin’ out here in the storm forever y’know. We better get indoors!”

“Yes dear, but somepony here has decided she would prefer- Oof!” Rarity suddenly is knocked back a little by Applejack emerging out from under the picnic table. “This here table ain’t doing much for me now with how bad it’s gotten! Despite mah concerns, we best head to Twilight’s place. I hope she’s got some safety measures in that there tree of her’s!”

The earth pony tips her hat down and immediately breaks into a gallop towards the library; the unicorn attempts to argue, but Applejack is out of earshot. Without any better option, she immediately goes after her.

“…and that’s the story of how I hatched Spike’s egg in my entrance exam.”

“How interesting!” Portal says, slowly tapping a hoof against her chin as she thinks. “And you really don’t know where Celestia got that egg from?”

“Very little about it, I have some theories… but, maybe one day I’ll-“

BANG.

The distinct sound of the Golden Oak Library’s door being forced opened rings out from the first floor; curious, Portal and Twilight immediately get up from their pillows and trot down the stairs to investigate - the unicorn leading, as Portal Bump chooses to instead peek her head before going any further.

“Oh! Applejack and Rarity!” Twilight exclaims, seeing the two mares piled on top of eachother, panting and wet from the torrential downpour.

Applejack grabs her hat and places it back on her head, shaking her head and glancing up at the purple unicorn. “Howdy Twilight, I hope ya don’t mind the intrusion. Me and Rares here were caught up in the rain, and I figured we’d take shelter here till it passes.”

“I must say though, Applejack had informed me on the dangers of being inside a tree during a storm like this one. I presume it’s covered, darling?”

“I have a magical lightning rod protecting the place, it’s safe here.” Twilight says proudly. Portal slowly continues down the staircase, glancing awkwardly back and forth. She suddenly lets out a frightened ‘eep’ when the voice of the apple-flanked mare cuts through. “Now who’s this you got here, Twilight?” Applejacks says, pointing at Portal Bump, who stumbles and yelps as she falls off the staircase from the side, thankfully using her wings to slow her fall and land safely upright.

Twilight looks at Portal and giggles.

“Sorry Applejack, I think you might’ve frightened her.”

“What, she somepony like Fluttershy?” The earth pony tilts her head as she sees the pegasus hide behind Twilight. Portal Bump proceeds to poke her head out.

“N-No I’m not shy… j-just. Um, I want to make good first impressions and… so far I haven’t done that well.” Portal gives a forced wide smile, her eyes betraying her attempted front of confidence.

“I think falling from the sky and crashing in front of me is up there in memorable introductions. Not a good one! But I’m not forgetting it anytime soon.” Twilight chuckles.

The pegasus glances to the side and huffs, puffing her wing feathers a little as she looks embarrassed about the fact.

“Well, I must ask - I’m sure you’re a good friend, Portal - but do you have any unused hair combs laying around, Twilight?” Rarity asks, seeming to rummage around the Golden Oak Library’s ground floor, pulling any books and opening any drawers she can find in the hopes of a misplaced comb.

“Upstairs, Rarity.” replies the other unicorn.

“Why thank you! Of course, I wouldn’t be in such a horrible predicament had the storm not come by to ruin my fabulous looks!”

“Yeah, the pegasus ponies really have outdone themselves with this storm.” Twilight replies, glancing out a nearby window. “Given the scale of it, I’ve already let Portal stay here; I figure I should extend the invite to the rest of my best friends!”

“Your generosity will not go unnoticed, Twilight.” Applejack replies, tipping her hat. Rarity immediately cuts in; “And I, who bears the Element of Generosity, say you should go wash your dirty hooves! Hmph!” and pompously trots up the staircase to the second floor, Applejack glaring at her the whole way through. “‘Ah swear, if ah’ve gotta spend another second with that pony…” She mutters to herself.


Portal seems to be stuck thinking about the way Twilight phrased what she said…

She called them the rest of her best friends..? As if… I’m…-


“So, this a friend of yours ya haven’t told us about?” Applejack says, trotting on over to get a closer look at the pegasus, who awkwardly waves hello, having been jolted from her thought process.

“I met Portal Bump a week or so ago, and it turns out she’s interested in the same fields of studies I am, meaning we ended up making good friends!”

“Heh, well any friend of Twilight is a friend of mine! Howdy, Portal!” The earth pony says, raising a hoof for a hoofshake, only to notice the muddiness still on it. “Oh, right, ah’m sorry about that.” Applejack chuckles. “Got a hose outside or something I could use, Twi?”

“Just use my bathroom, lest you need to go out in the rain again.” Twilight replies, pointing towards the appropriate door. The earth pony nods and trots off to get her hooves clean.

Portal Bump watches as she leaves, and then turns back to face Twilight. “You’ve got… an interesting selection of friends, Twilight. I mean, you had told me about them a bunch already, but seeing them…” Portal motions outwards with her hooves. “Y’know!! In the flesh! Is like… You get it, right?!”

Twilight chuckles as the portal pony verbally trips over her words, but just as she is about to reply, the distinct sound of the stopwatch cuts through, and a shriek pierces through the library from upstairs immediately after.

Beep-Beep-Beep! Beep-Beep-Beep!

“Stop that infernal noise! Oh goodness, where is it coming from?!”

Beep-Beep-Beep! Beep-Beep-Beep!

The pegasus and purple unicorn giggle. Rarity comes galloping down the steps with her hair comb stuck in her mane, exasperated. “What a cruel prank, Twilight! I expected better of you!” The white unicorn says, Portal can’t help but look to the side as she tries to stop her laughter from increasing.

“Rarity. That was just a stopwatch me and Portal left running, I forgot to pause it when you and Applejack came in.” Twilight clarifies. “Now that I’ve got three best friends here though…oh, we could do a sleepover! All four of us!” She continues, gleefully tip-tapping her hooves against the ground.


She just called you one of her best friends. Ok. Yup. Yea. Okay. Ahuh. Okay. Act natural Portal, act natural-


“YEA! GIRLS NIGHT!” Portal shouts, leaping into the air and pumping a hoof.

Applejack lets out a sigh of relief as she exits the bathroom, her hooves - and seemingly any muddy hoofprints she left in the library - are all cleaned up. Not spotting any of the three on the first floor, she trots up towards the second floor of the Golden Oak Library.

She gasps and furrows her eyebrows as she catches Rarity and Twilight applying mud masks to each other. Applejack immediately interrogates the two unicorns. “Now wait just a minute here! ‘Ah went to wash my hooves of mah own accord, get mahself nice and clean to be a good houseguest after ‘ah was so kindly offered to use the bath here, and now y’all are getting messy on purpose?” The earth pony finishes, raising an eyebrow.

“It’s just what is called a mud mask, dear!” Rarity says. “It’s meant to refresh and rejuvenate your complexion…” She continues, before Twilight adds on with “…and it means we get to give each other makeovers! Eeehee! Says so in the book!”

Applejack takes note of something however - there’s only three of them present here, but Twilight just earlier had made sure to introduce that new portal pony to them both as a friend of hers…

“So why ain’t that Portal mare part of this?” The apple pony questions, raising an eyebrow at the two unicorns. The question makes them realise something as the pair look at eachother - where had Portal gone? She was there just a bit ago and fully eager and ready to be part of this, but just as things were getting prepared… she just vanished.

Twilight nervously glances around. “Oh gosh, where did she go..?”


Her ear flicks as she suddenly hears the soft sound of the portal pony replying through chattering teeth.


“I-I’m still here…”


The trio are confused, looking around to try to locate the source of Portal Bump’s voice, but it takes Applejack glancing upwards to finally get the answer to that.

“Now what in all of Equestria…?” Applejack says with a hint of confusion - Portal, mud mask on her face, appears to have clung to the ceiling, shivering as she goes back into a little alcove high up in the library’s structure. “Portal, dear, what has gotten into you?” The white unicorn questions, concerned about just what may have gotten them so riled up…

…only for Twilight to remember something that Portal Bump had told her some time ago.

“Rarity, this is going to sound weird, but…” Twilight’s horn lights up, levitating some slices of a certain vegetable they had used as part of the makeovers.


“Portal Bump is afraid of cucumbers.”


The other two mares stare in bewilderment.


Applejack has to press a hoof against her mouth to stifle her laughter at the absurdity of it. Twilight glares disapprovingly at Rarity as she lets a small crack of laughter slip. “Look, I get it sounds funny, but Portal has good reason for it.” She says, using her magic to dispose of the slices of gourd that seem to irrationally terrify the portal pony. “Even I don’t fully understand it, but the way I see it, if a friend has a problem, we should try to be accommodating, should we not?”

“...’Ah suppose that’s right.” The apple mare says, watching as the pegasus slowly descends down to the first floor, giving the other three ponies a look of embarrassment. Applejack trots over and slinks a reassuring hoof around Portal. “‘Ah understand your plight, Portal. Hay, makes complete sense to me that Twi over here would be so considerate…”

She smugly turns to glance at Twilight, and then back at Portal Bump.

“‘Ah mean, her reaction that one time to a ladybug who was just strollin’ on by…”

“I thought you promised to not bring that up..!” Twilight says sheepishly, as her face goes red with embarrassment. Rarity can’t help but break into a laugh. “Goodness Twilight, a fear of ladybugs? Now that gives some context as to why you avoided my boutique for a day after I unveiled that dress inspired by one!”

“Portaldoyouhavethebookonyou?” The purple unicorn says rapidly, attempting to switch the subject as fast as she can away from her growing embarrassment. Portal Bump luckily has it with her, held under one of her wings - Slumber 101: All You've Ever Wanted to Know About Slumber Parties But Were Afraid to Ask - and nods.

“Yup! We should be sticking to the schedule, right?” The pegasus replies. “Even, if, uhm. I uh. Have to… sit out of this one.” She says, nervously tapping two hooves together, Twilight nods and pulls over a soaked washcloth to remove the mud mask from Portal’s face.

“Sorry, I just, uhm… Look, nothing cucumber for me, okay? They’re just so… gourd-y.”

“You’re okay, Portal. Besides, ideally we need to make sure to do everything by the book - even if not everypony partakes - in order to make my very first slumber party officially fun!” Twilight says happily.

“So, pretty please, dear Applejack, will you be a dear and partake in the makeovers in place of Portal?” The other unicorn adds, tilting her head with a big smile, her eyes fluttering, before she quickly then adds; “A pony like you could use some cleanliness in your life.”

Applejack glares at the two unicorns, her eyes moving back and forth between them, before sighing.

“Fine.”

Twilight seems mostly oblivious to the growing tension between the other two mares as she and Portal look through the book on slumber parties, the pegasus having comfortably sat on a pillow pile.

“…but understand this Rares, ‘ahm only doing this because ‘ah ain’t intent on ruining Twilight’s very first slumber party. You wouldn’t want to do anythin’ that ruins it now will ya?”

“Of course not! I trust you wouldn’t either, darling?”

“You betcha.”

“I presume we have an agreement then?”

“You’ve got a deal, Rares.” Applejack spits on her hoof and reaches it out to the white unicorn. “Seal the deal then.”

Rarity grimaces and raises a hoof that she crosses against her chest. “Oh how gross! You know, there’s messy, and then there’s just plain rude.”

“There’s fussy, and then there’s just plain ol’ getting on somepony’s nerves…”

The two glare at each other, gazes fixated, but before the next part of the confrontation can continue, they’re interrupted by the two eggheads giggling to each other.

“Oh gosh, could you imagine if we did this one? Ghost stories would be great!” Portal gleefully says as she points at an entry in the Slumber Party guide book, kicking her hindlegs in a cycling motion with Twilight taking the book out of her hooves.

“We absolutely should! Buuuut let’s get these makeovers dealt with first to check it off.”

Twilight and Portal glance over to see Rarity preparing Applejack - with the mud mask applied to her face…


…and then a cucumber slice comes into view.


Portal Bump rockets off her pillows and right into the ceiling at lightning speed, audibly crashing into the roof of the Golden Oak Library.

Rarity realises what she’s done as the sound of the pegasus’ impact emanates through the library, and they turn around to sheepishly smile at Twilight, who is standing over the white unicorn with an eyebrow raised.

“…and they say, at night, if you listen closely… you can still hear the screams of all their replicas! Functionally indistinguishable-“

“Hey, wait a minute!”

Click.

“You’re just telling the ghost story that appears in the second Tales of Aperature book!” Twilight says, her magic pulling a nearby copy of it from the shelves and flipping it open as she pulls it towards herself. “Yeah, right here! When Narbacular Drop is being guided by Wheat Fields through the darkness in Chapter 5, he tells her this story to pass the time and recount what he knows about where they are!”

…Portal’s face is covered in a growing embarrassed blush - either she’s blushing because she hadn’t expected the unicorn to know that much about her favourite books, or she’s embarrassed that she had been called out on taking something from it.


It’s probably both.


“I mean, it counts for ghost stories now doesn’t it? The Headless Horse legend you told is one that’s existed for a long time! The book never specified we had to come up with an original ghost story…”

“…I guess you’re right.”

“Why don’t you try now, darling?”

The way she excitedly jumps at the chance to do anything for Twilight, the way she’s been nerding out with her, even the way the tone of her voice subtly shifts whenever in conversation with that purple unicorn…

Oh Sweet Celestia. Oh no. Oh I-I’m… I’m gonna have to answer this. It’s the rules. And I can’t break the rules. Especially during Girl’s Night…

Feelings? Twilight thinks.
