Description

Shenanigans ensue when a scheduled storm by the Pegasi make Portal Bump’s visit to the Golden Oak Library into an impromptu sleepover consisting of herself, Twilight Sparkle, Applejack and Rarity.

Opposites, Parallels & Slumber Parties is another story in the PortalSparkle AU, this one aims to adapt the Season 1 Episode Look Before You Sleep with the new variable of Portal Bump added into the equation and the shenanigans that come from her involvement.

Readers are expected to have some familiarity with the episode's events before reading this story.

The PortalSparkle AU is a very self-indulgent New Game+ for the series, focusing on introducing new characters and exploring new stories in Equestria & beyond that occur alongside the main series of events while still respecting the universe, characters & lore setup in the normal Friendship is Magic. Unless stated otherwise, you can expect things to play out much similarly to the normal show.

If you dislike the coloured dialogue or find it difficult to read, there is a completely black & white version of the fanfiction available to read here.

Yes, it's an OC x Canon AU.
